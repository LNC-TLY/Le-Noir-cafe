Thalassery and Kannur have a food culture shaped by generations of tradition, coastal ingredients and unforgettable recipes.

From fragrant biryani to the much-loved Mappila snacks, Malabar cuisine has a character all its own.

Thalassery Biryani

02 — COASTAL SPECIALITY

Soft rice-flour flatbread, a familiar favourite across North Kerala.
